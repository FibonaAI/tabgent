# Tabgent use cases

[← Home](../README.md) · **English** · [简体中文](use-cases.zh-CN.md)

Collecting invoices at month end, entering your resume again, or remembering the details of several rental listings can mean a lot of switching between pages and files.

Open the relevant pages and tell Tabgent your goal, which materials to use, and where you want it to stop. Adapt the requests below to your situation.

**These uses are still being evaluated; complete workflows across different websites have not been verified.** Start with one website, one form or a few pages.

## Collect monthly invoices without hunting through every account

Invoices live in different service accounts. Every month, you have to find the billing section, select a period, download files and organize them for accounting. Ask Tabgent to help locate and organize the requested records, with a separate list of anything missing.

> Find last month's invoices in these signed-in accounts. Download them, name them by date and supplier, and summarize the amounts. List anything missing, and don't substitute billing-page screenshots for invoices.

Specify the accounts, month and destination folder. Take over when sign-in verification is needed, then check the original files against the summary.

## Apply for a job without entering your resume all over again

You have uploaded a resume, but the website still asks for education, experience and dates. Give Tabgent your resume and ask it to help with the current form, leaving unanswered questions for you.

> Fill this job application using my resume. Keep the experience and dates as written, and don't add skills I haven't listed. Ask me about missing information. Let me review the form before submitting.

You can supply answers or correct details as it works. Personally confirm declarations such as work authorization.

## Compare rental listings without losing track of the details

One listing has lower rent but charges for parking. Another is in the right location but does not allow pets. A third leaves out the move-in date. Ask Tabgent to organize candidates against your requirements, keeping uncertainties visible so you can decide whom to contact.

> Compare these open rental listings against my budget and move-in requirements below. List rent, extra charges, availability dates and pet rules. Mark unclear details as unconfirmed and list questions for the landlords.

Include your budget, move-in date and non-negotiable requirements. For a commute comparison, add the destination and travel mode. Undisclosed charges and actual availability still need confirmation with the landlord.

## Plan a child's holiday activities and spot uncovered dates

An activity may look appealing but have the wrong age limits, session dates or pickup times. Extended-care charges can also change the cost. Open your candidates and ask Tabgent to compare them with your family's schedule.

> Compare these activities using my child's age, holiday dates, pickup times and budget. Make a weekly plan showing uncovered dates, extra care charges and registration links. Don't register yet.

Recheck places and current arrangements before booking. Unpublished information should remain unknown rather than be filled in with last year's dates.

## Find out what your college and scholarship applications still need

Every program has different essay prompts, word limits, recommendations and deadlines. Ask Tabgent to compare the official requirements with your existing materials and make a checklist you can work through.

> Compare these official program pages with my documents. List eligibility, deadlines, essay prompts and word limits. Sort by the earliest deadline and tell me what each application still needs.

Specify the application year and distinguish prepared files from submitted applications. Unclear eligibility and conflicting dates need further confirmation.

## Organize the paperwork after a disrupted flight

Rebooking notices, itineraries and receipts are scattered, and you still need to find the right refund or compensation route. Ask Tabgent to organize the facts and documents before reviewing an application draft.

> Build a timeline from my itinerary, airline notices and receipts. Find the official application route, list missing documents and help prepare a form draft. Let me check it before submission.

Provide the full itinerary so an individual flight's delay is not confused with the final arrival time. Eligibility and any refund or compensation amount depend on applicable rules; a prepared draft does not establish an entitlement.

## Find out how to stop an unwanted subscription

The cancellation option may be hard to find, and you may not know whether cancelling ends access immediately. Ask Tabgent to find the process and explain the effects before you decide to proceed.

> Find this subscription's cancellation option. First tell me the effective date, remaining access and any charges shown. Wait for my confirmation before taking action, then save the cancellation result.

Specify whether you subscribed through the website or an app store. A browser cannot complete a process that requires phone or in-person contact. Check the final account status or confirmation to establish the result.

## Start with one task you already need to do

Give a clear goal, such as organizing a few invoices or completing the current application. Provide the relevant pages or files and say where Tabgent should stop. You can add instructions, change direction or stop the task while it works.
